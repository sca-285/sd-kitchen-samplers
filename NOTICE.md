## Changes made to the included code

The generated files under `lib_kitchen/canon/` differ from their sources only by the fixed textual rewrites listed at the top of `tools/vendor.py`. Rerunning the tool against the WebUI commits named in each file's header reproduces them byte for byte.

All other changes are listed in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md). They include bug fixes to Extra Samplers' Euler Multipass CFG++, Heun Ancestral and SSPRK3, and to reForge's schedule types.

## Relation to sd-forge-extra-samplers

This project is not a fork of MisterChief95's Extra Samplers, and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with it. It includes the samplers that are unique to that extension, with credit, so that one extension can serve Forge, reForge and Neo together.

That extension's own copies of algorithms the WebUIs already ship were not taken: Euler Dy and its variants, Gradient Estimation, the Res Multistep family, and its RES4LYF port. The WebUI's version is used instead.
